We show that divergence-free vector fields admit parsimonious representations, extending the classical curl representation beyond three dimensions. Building on this, we propose a computationally efficient network architecture that is divergence-free by design. On self-supervised image denoising it sets a new benchmark for constraint-based methods under Gaussian, Poisson and real-world noise, closing the gap with leading denoisers.
