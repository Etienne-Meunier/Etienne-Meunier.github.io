We introduce a resource-efficient neural network architecture with zero divergence by design, adapted to high-dimensional problems. Applied to self-supervised image denoising through Stein's unbiased risk estimation, it is competitive with other divergence-based approaches and outperforms them when the noise level is unknown and varies across the training data.
