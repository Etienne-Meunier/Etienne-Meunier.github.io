We leverage the autodifferentiable version of the JAX-based Veros ocean model I developed in earlier work to turn model tuning into gradient-based optimization. We calibrate physical parameters against surface and domain-integrated targets, and train neural subgrid parameterizations and model-error corrections online through the solver. Parameters optimized over about 100 solver steps restore the simulated circulation over integration times three orders of magnitude longer, a pioneering approach to tuning parameterizations with differentiable models in a full ocean model.
