We propose a continuous-time Koopman autoencoder whose linear latent dynamics can be integrated in closed form at any horizon in a single step. It gives stable, fast long-term forecasts (110x faster than diffusion baselines) and makes data assimilation through the emulator cheap and accurate, with a cost independent of the assimilation window.
