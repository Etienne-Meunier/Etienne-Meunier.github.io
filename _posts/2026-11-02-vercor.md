We propose a JAX library to couple Earth system model components with each other and with data models. It combines a simple, fully differentiable coupler with a Bayesian optimizer that automatically tunes the model configuration. We illustrate it by coupling an ocean to an atmosphere model and tuning the result, and show that the coupled model is differentiable end to end, so gradient-based methods can also be used for parameter optimization.
